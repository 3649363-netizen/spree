Spree Commerce gives you a powerful, flexible platform to build and manage your online store - but before you can start selling, there are a few key steps to complete. 

This guide walks you through the essential setup tasks, from configuring payments and taxes to adding products and launching your storefront. Whether you're launching an MVP or just exploring Spree’s capabilities, this guide will help you get up and running quickly.

## Getting Started

![Getting Started tab showing the onboarding checklist with four key tasks](/images/user/quickstart/1-getting-started.png)

When you log into a fresh Spree Commerce instance, the dashboard shows a Getting Started checklist to help you configure the essentials and go live faster.

The default checklist is ordered by shipping address, payment methods, products, support email, taxes, and storefront connection. Extensions can add or remove tasks, so the list may differ in your store. Completed tasks stay marked until all applicable tasks are complete; the widget then disappears. Let’s walk through the main setup steps below.

### Setup Payment Method

![Payment setup onboarding step](/images/user/quickstart/2-setup-payments.png)

To sell anything, you’ll need to configure at least one payment method.

Click **Setup Payment Method** to jump to **Settings → Payments**.

![Payments section of admin dashboard](/images/user/quickstart/3-settings-payments.png)

The table lists your configured payment methods, including their provider, storefront visibility and status. Drag a row to change the order customers see at checkout.

<Note>To add another method, click **Add payment method**. The provider selector only lists providers installed in your application. If you expect a provider that is not listed, ask your developer to confirm it has been added to the codebase.</Note>

Choose the provider, enter its required settings and credentials, set its name and storefront visibility, then click **Create payment method**.

<Note>Most payment gateways require you to have an account with the provider before setup.</Note>

To learn more about available methods and configuration, see [<u>Payments</u>](https://spreecommerce.org/docs/user/settings/payments).

### Add Products

![Add products Spree onboarding step](/images/user/quickstart/4-add-products.png)

There are three ways to populate your store’s catalog:

- [<u>Add products manually</u>](https://spreecommerce.org/docs/user/products/adding-products) (ideal for small catalogs or testing)
- Import products using CSV (essential for large catalogs)
- [<u>Invite vendors to add their own products</u>](https://spreecommerce.org/docs/user/sellers/onboarding) (for marketplaces - Spree Enterprise only)

Click the **Add Products** button to start adding manually, or refer to the relevant articles based on your method.

Once you’ve added some products, make sure to set them as **Active** to publish them on the storefront.

For more detailed guides on creating and managing products, see:

- [<u>Creating Products</u>](https://spreecommerce.org/docs/user/products/adding-products)
- [<u>Editing Products</u>](https://spreecommerce.org/docs/user/products/adding-products)
- [<u>Editing Multi-Variant Products</u>](https://spreecommerce.org/docs/user/products/variants)
- [<u>Managing Products in Bulk</u>](https://spreecommerce.org/docs/user/products/products)

### Add Customer Support Email

![Add a customer support email onboarding step](/images/user/quickstart/5-customer-support-email.png)

This small but critical step ensures customers know how to reach you.

Simply enter your customer support email in the field provided, and click **Save**.

This address will appear on your storefront and works as a contact for your customers in case they have any queries or require support.

### Setup Taxes Collection

![Setup tax collection ecommerce onboarding step](/images/user/quickstart/6-setup-taxes.png)

To charge the correct taxes at checkout, click **Setup Tax Rates** to be taken to **Settings → Taxes**.

From here, you can configure:

- Tax rates (manual or through third-party services like Avalara)
- Tax categories

For full instructions, please refer to our [<u>Tax Rates support article</u>](/user/settings/tax-rates).

## Delivery Setup

Physical products need a delivery method before customers can complete checkout. Open **Settings → Delivery Profiles** and choose the store default profile, or create a separate profile for a group of products. Set the locations the goods ship from, the zones they can reach, and the delivery methods offered in each zone.

See [<u>Delivery Profiles</u>](/user/settings/delivery-profiles) for the full setup, including zones, rates and delivery methods.

<Note>For digital products, use a delivery profile with its kind set to **Digital**. The profile’s capabilities and delivery methods determine which digital fulfillment options are available.</Note>
